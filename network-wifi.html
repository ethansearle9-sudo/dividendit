The problem

A slow network is a tax on every person, every day.

Calls that break up. Files that take an age to open. A system that freezes at the busiest time of day. These get blamed on the software or the internet provider, and the real cause is often the network in between.

Many business networks grew without a plan. A switch added here, a cheap access point there, cables nobody has labelled. It mostly works, until the business grows or one device fails and nobody knows what was connected to it.

A network should be designed, documented and watched, the same as any other system the business depends on.

What it's worth

The foundation everything else stands on

Every cloud application, phone call and business system depends on the network. Getting it right lifts the performance of everything above it.

  • It lifts productivity. Systems respond faster and calls stop dropping, for everyone, all day.
  • It avoids wasted spend. A proper diagnosis often shows that the costly upgrade someone suggested was never the problem.
  • It supports growth. New people, new sites and new systems can be added without starting again.
Is it right for you?

An honest fit check

A good fit if

  • Your team complains about slow systems or poor call quality
  • The network has grown piece by piece without a plan
  • Nobody has a current diagram of what is connected to what
  • You are opening, moving or expanding a site

Probably not the right fit if

  • You need a home network set up
  • You want equipment supplied without design or management
Questions owners ask

Frequently asked questions

What are managed network services?

An outside team designs, monitors, maintains and supports your business network, including switches, routers, firewall and WiFi, for a predictable fee.

How do I know if the network is the cause of our slow systems?

We measure it. A survey shows where the delay actually is, whether that is the internet connection, the WiFi, a switch or the application itself.

Do we need to replace all our network equipment?

Rarely. We keep what is fit for purpose and replace what is holding you back, in order of impact.

Can you manage networks across several sites?

Yes. We design and manage multi-site networks so that each location has the same experience and can reach shared systems securely.
