The problem

Two businesses, two of everything.

On the day a deal completes you own two email systems, two finance systems, two sets of suppliers and two ways of doing everything. The savings in the investment case depend on turning two into one.

That rarely happens by itself. Without a plan, the two halves run side by side for years, paying for both, while staff work around the gaps. Or integration is rushed, and customers feel it.

Technology is where integration succeeds or stalls. It deserves a plan before the deal completes, with costs and risks understood while they can still affect the price.

What it's worth

The deal thesis depends on it

Most acquisitions are justified by savings and growth that require the two businesses to work as one. Technology is the mechanism.

  • It protects the price you paid. Unexpected integration costs come straight out of the return.
  • It brings savings forward. Every month of running two of everything is a month of synergy lost.
  • It reduces risk. The acquired business is brought up to your security standard before its systems are connected to yours.

Questions owners ask

Frequently asked questions

What is M&A IT integration?

It is the work of combining the technology of two businesses after an acquisition: email and identities, networks, business systems, data, suppliers and support, so that they operate as one.

When should IT integration planning start?

Before the deal completes. The earlier it starts, the more accurately costs can be reflected in the price and the sooner savings are delivered.

How long does IT integration take?

It depends on the size and complexity of the two businesses. Essentials such as email and security are usually aligned early, and core business systems take longer. We agree a realistic plan before work starts.

Should we always fully integrate an acquired business?

Not always. Sometimes the right answer is to integrate the foundation, such as security, email and support, and leave a specialist business system alone. We advise case by case, with the numbers.
