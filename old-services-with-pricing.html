Per-user pricing covers your people. If your business has servers, firewalls, or managed network infrastructure, these are quoted separately on a per-asset basis — at a fixed, transparent price. See the infrastructure pricing below. All contracts are monthly rolling — no lock-in.

Infrastructure management

Your environment, priced fairly.

Servers, firewalls, and network infrastructure vary by business. We quote each customer's environment separately — on a fixed, standardised price list. No surprises, no custom negotiations.

Every business has a different environment — a retail business might have just a firewall and 12 laptops; a manufacturing site might have three servers, a firewall stack, 20 switches, and 40 access points. We don't try to fold that variability into the per-user price. Instead, per-user tiers cover your people; per-asset add-ons cover your infrastructure — at a fixed rate that's fast to quote and easy to understand.

Hardware — servers, switches, laptops, cabling, UPS — is always a one-off project cost, quoted separately from ongoing management.

How we start

NIS2-secure in two weeks.
Not two months.

Most customers come to us with existing equipment, no Active Directory setup, and a mix of M365 licences. That's the standard starting point — not the exception. Here's how we handle it.

01
Week 1–2

Secure Foundation

Pre-onboarding call to collect credentials and document your environment. N-central agent deployed to all devices — remotely, no AD required. Huntress EDR live. MFA enforced. DNS filtering on. Backup configured. Licences audited and aligned.

02
Month 1–3

Stabilise

Patch management automated. Security awareness training deployed. Firewall and network reviewed — infrastructure add-ons quoted if needed. Gap report produced, showing your position against full NIS2 compliance. Gap report becomes the natural conversation for moving to Operate or Grow.

03
Month 6+

Optimise

Workflow review with you and your team. Technology roadmap built around your goals. Private cloud assessment if relevant. Annual penetration test scheduled. From here, technology is actively building value — not just keeping the lights on.

🛡️

No Active Directory? No problem. The majority of businesses we onboard don't have an existing AD or Entra ID setup. Our deployment playbook handles this — N-central network discovery finds all devices, and we push the agent using local admin credentials. You're secured before we even begin the infrastructure conversation.
