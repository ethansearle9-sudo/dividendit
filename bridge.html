Modelled effect—
Measured: outage minutes from monitoring, ticket volume and handle time, device boot and logon telemetry. Hard numbers.

Assumed: that recovered time becomes output. It doesn't, entirely, which is what the realisation factor is for. Drop it to 40% and the figure moves with it. Better to show you the dial than bury it in a total.

Why contribution, not revenue: an hour returned produces the margin on that hour's work, not the top line. Valuing it at revenue overstates it by roughly four times.

Why only the premium: a breach that didn't happen can't be priced honestly. The premium delta is a real price, quoted by an underwriter who saw the evidence. That's the one security number that belongs in a P&L conversation.
