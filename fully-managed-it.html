The problem

You did not start a business to manage IT.

Somewhere along the way, IT became your job, or your office manager's. One supplier for the internet, another for the phones, someone for the laptops, a reseller for Microsoft and the vendor of the system the business actually runs on. You are the glue between them, and every problem ends up on your desk.

The real cost is not on any invoice. It is the hour you lose each time, the minutes your team loses every day to slow systems and login problems, and the risks nobody is managing because nobody owns the whole picture.

Managed IT services means paying a fixed monthly fee to have your technology looked after proactively, instead of paying by the hour when something breaks. Fully managed means we become your IT department, and we are accountable for the lot.

What it's worth

What it is worth to an owner

We look at every technology decision twice: what it costs, and what it does to earnings and risk, the two things a buyer uses to value your business.

  • Your time back. IT problems stop reaching the owner. That hour goes back to the work only you can do.
  • Less daily friction. Fifteen minutes a day lost to slow systems is roughly 60 hours a year for each person.
  • A business that is easier to sell. Documented systems, evidenced controls and predictable costs are what technology due diligence looks for.
Is it right for you?

An honest fit check

A good fit if

  • You have no internal IT team, or IT is somebody's side job
  • Every IT problem eventually reaches you
  • You use several suppliers and nobody owns the whole picture
  • You want one predictable monthly cost

Probably not the right fit if

  • You have an internal IT person you want to keep. Co-managed IT is built for that
  • You only want someone to call, and pay by the hour, when something breaks
  • You have fewer than ten people. We may not be the most cost-effective fit
Questions owners ask

Frequently asked questions

What are managed IT services?

Managed IT services means an outside team looks after your technology proactively for a fixed monthly fee. It covers support for your people, monitoring and maintenance of devices and servers, security, backup and planning.

What is the difference between managed IT services and outsourced IT support?

They describe the same thing. Managed IT services is the industry's term. Most business owners simply call it outsourced IT support or an outsourced IT company.

How much do managed IT services cost?

They are priced per user per month, all inclusive. The figure depends on your headcount and the service level you need. Our cost calculator lets you compare it with employing in-house IT, and we give you a fixed price after a short conversation.

How long does it take to switch IT provider?

The security baseline is in place within two weeks and most businesses are fully onboarded within 30 days. We handle the handover from your current provider so that you do not have to referee it.

Do you come on site?

Most issues are fixed remotely within minutes. Scheduled visits and on-site callouts are part of our Operate and Grow service levels.
