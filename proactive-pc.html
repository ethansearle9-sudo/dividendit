The problem

Break and fix is the expensive way to run IT.

The traditional model is simple: something breaks, you call someone, they fix it and send an invoice. It feels economical because you only pay when there is a problem.

But a computer rarely fails without notice. The disk fills up, the updates stop installing, the machine gets slower month by month. By the time someone calls for help, hours have already been lost, usually spread thinly across weeks where nobody counted them.

Proactive support turns that around. We watch the health of every device and fix what we see before your people notice.

What it's worth

The fifteen minutes nobody counts

Slow, unreliable computers are a cost that never shows up as a line item, which is exactly why it goes unmanaged.

  • It recovers lost time. Fifteen minutes a day per person is roughly 60 hours a year each. Across 40 people that is more than one full-time salary.
  • It smooths spending. Planned replacement over several years beats a surprise bill when five laptops fail in one month.
  • It lowers risk. Up-to-date, encrypted devices are much harder to attack and much less costly to lose.

Questions owners ask

Frequently asked questions

What is proactive IT support?

It is support that monitors and maintains your devices continuously to prevent problems, as opposed to waiting for something to break and then fixing it.

Will monitoring slow our computers down?

No. The management agent is lightweight and runs in the background. Maintenance is scheduled for times that do not interrupt work.

How long should a business laptop last?

It depends on the work it does, but most businesses plan on replacing laptops every few years. We track the age and health of each device and tell you when replacement makes more sense than repair.

Can you see our files or what our staff are doing?

The tools monitor the health and security of the device, not the content of people's work. Remote access to a screen is used for support and with the user's knowledge.
