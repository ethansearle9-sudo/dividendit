The problem

Security is a full-time job being done part-time.

A good IT manager is a generalist by necessity. They fix the printer, onboard the new starter, renew the licences and manage the suppliers. Security is one job among thirty, and it is the one that never shouts until something has already gone wrong.

It also does not stop at five o'clock. One person cannot watch alerts through the night, take a holiday and keep up with how attacks are changing.

Co-managed security is not a replacement for your team. It gives them specialists to lean on, tools they could not justify alone and cover when they are not there.

What it's worth

Stronger security without a bigger payroll

Hiring a second IT person does not give you security cover around the clock. Sharing a specialist team does, for a fraction of the cost.

  • It protects your investment in people. Your IT team spends their time on work that moves the business, not on watching dashboards.
  • It removes key-person risk. Security no longer depends on one person being available and up to date.
  • It gives leadership evidence. Reporting that shows the board, an insurer or a buyer what is in place and working.
Is it right for you?

An honest fit check

A good fit if

  • You have invested in an internal IT person or team and want to keep them
  • They are stretched, and security is not getting the attention it needs
  • You want cover outside office hours and during holidays
  • You want an outside view on risk that reports to leadership

Probably not the right fit if

  • You have no internal IT at all. Fully managed is the better fit
  • You want to replace your IT team rather than strengthen it
  • You want a one-off review. A risk assessment is the place to start
Questions owners ask

Frequently asked questions

Will co-managed security replace our IT person?

No. The model is built around keeping them. They stay in charge of day-to-day IT, and we take on the specialist security work and the hours they cannot cover.

Who does what during an incident?

That is agreed in writing before anything happens. Typically we detect, contain and investigate, your team handles on-site actions and communication with staff, and one named person on your side makes the business decisions.

Can our team use your tools?

Yes. Your IT team gets access to the monitoring and security tooling for your environment, so they are working from the same information we are.

What happens if our IT person leaves?

Because your environment is documented and monitored by us, the security side carries on unchanged. We can also step up to cover day-to-day IT while you decide what to do next.
