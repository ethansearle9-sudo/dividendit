Public cloud, where it wins

Elastic demand, unpredictable growth, global reach: that is what public cloud is for. Our job is to put the right workloads there and keep the cost governed, not just billed. We advise on placement, migrate, manage and control spend across Microsoft Azure and AWS. Cloud is a placement decision, not a religion.

The problem

For a decade, the answer to every question has been “move it to the cloud”.

For some workloads that answer is right. A customer-facing product, a seasonal spike or a new system whose demand nobody can predict are all exactly what elastic, rented capacity is for. Email, files and collaboration belong there too. The economics of hosting those yourself ended years ago.

But rented compute is expensed. It comes off your earnings every month, and earnings are the number your valuation multiple is applied to. For systems that do the same job every day, renting forever is often the expensive choice.

Then there is waste. Industry surveys consistently put wasted cloud spend at around a quarter of the bill. The discipline is deciding workload by workload, with the numbers on the table, and then governing what you rent.

What it's worth

The honest conversation about cloud

We have no reason to push you into the cloud or out of it. The right answer is the one that gives the same result for the lowest total cost.

  • It protects earnings. Every unnecessary cloud charge is a recurring cost multiplied at exit.
  • It buys flexibility where it counts. Pay for elasticity where demand really varies.
  • It avoids lock-in by accident. Placement decisions made deliberately are much easier to change later.
Is it right for you?

An honest fit check

A good fit if

  • Your cloud bill keeps rising and nobody can explain why
  • You have been told to move everything to the cloud and want a second opinion
  • You have a workload with unpredictable or seasonal demand
  • You are replacing ageing servers and weighing up the options

Probably not the right fit if

  • You want the cheapest hosting with no management
  • You need software development on cloud platforms. We run the infrastructure, we do not write the application
Questions owners ask

Frequently asked questions

What is public cloud?

Public cloud is computing capacity rented from a large provider such as Microsoft Azure or AWS, shared across many customers and paid for by usage. You can scale it up and down quickly without buying hardware.

Is the cloud always cheaper?

No. It is usually cheaper for workloads that vary, and for commodity services such as email. For steady, predictable systems, owning the infrastructure often costs much less over its life. Our own vs rent analysis has the numbers.

How do you migrate to the cloud without disrupting the business?

With a plan that moves one workload at a time, tests each step and keeps a way back until the new environment is proven. The switch itself is done outside working hours.

Can you reduce our existing cloud bill?

Usually. Bring your last invoice and we will show you what is earning its keep and what is waste, such as oversized servers, forgotten test systems and storage nobody uses.

Azure or AWS?

For most businesses already using Microsoft 365, Azure is the natural fit because sign-in and management are shared. We manage both, and recommend based on the workload.
