Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

We are a cybersecurity business, so we hold your data to the standard we would hold our own. This page explains what we collect, why, who handles it for us and what you can ask us to do with it.

Who we are

Dividend IT is run by Ethan Searle in Co. Clare, Ireland. For anything on this page, including a request about your data, email ethan@dividendit.com.

What we collect and why

WhatWhyLawful basis
Your name, email and message when you use the contact formTo reply to youLegitimate interest in answering enquiries
Your email when you ask for the Owner’s GuideTo send the guide and a short series of follow up emails, which you can stop at any timeConsent
Your name, email and answers when you book a callTo arrange and prepare for the callSteps you ask us to take before a contract
Pages viewed, device type and approximate location (Google Analytics)To see which pages are useful and improve the siteConsent, only if you click Accept
Technical request data such as IP addressTo serve the site and protect it from abuseLegitimate interest in running a secure website

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising profiles.

Who handles it for us

We use a small number of providers who process data on our behalf under written agreements:

  • Formspree (contact and guide forms)
  • Calendly (call bookings)
  • Google (Workspace email, Analytics and the fonts on this site)
  • Netlify and Cloudflare (hosting, delivery and security)

Some of these providers are based outside the European Economic Area. Where data leaves the EEA it is protected by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries and booking details: up to two years after our last contact, unless you become a client.
  • Guide sign ups: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.
  • Analytics data: no longer than 14 months.

Cookies

Analytics cookies are only set if you click Accept on the cookie notice. You can change your choice at any time with cookie settings.

NamePurposeLasts
_ga, _ga_*Google Analytics: tells visits apart so we can count themUp to 2 years
ddit_consent (browser storage)Remembers your cookie choiceUntil you clear it

Your rights

You can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or take a copy with you. Where we rely on consent you can withdraw it at any time. Email ethan@dividendit.com and we will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission in Ireland, or the data protection authority where you live.

Changes

If we change how we use your data, we will update this page and the date at the top.
