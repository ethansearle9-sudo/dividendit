Everywhere else, resilience means backups. In Johannesburg it also means power, connectivity and the fact that your best technical people keep emigrating.

The Johannesburg problem

Three things make IT harder here, and none of them are in a standard support contract.

Power. Load shedding is an IT problem before it is an electrical one. Servers and network equipment that shut down abruptly and repeatedly fail earlier than they should, and a UPS that nobody has tested is a false comfort. Businesses that handle this well have planned the sequence: what stays up, for how long, and what happens when the batteries run out.

Connectivity. One fibre line is a single point of failure, and in this market that failure is not rare. Proper failover to a second path is normal practice here and a luxury elsewhere.

People. Skilled technical staff leave, for Cape Town, for the UK, for Australia. A business that depends on one excellent systems person is carrying a risk that has materialised repeatedly across this market for a decade.

On top of that sits POPIA, which places real obligations on how personal information is secured and what happens when it is not.

Questions owners ask

Frequently asked questions

How do you handle load shedding?

By planning it rather than reacting to it. We work out what must stay up and for how long, size the protection to that, make sure equipment shuts down cleanly when the batteries run out, and then test it. An untested UPS is the most common false comfort we find on site.

Do you provide connectivity failover?

We design and manage it. One fibre line is a single point of failure and in this market it fails. What matters is that the second path has been proven to take over, not that it exists on an invoice.

Our IT person is emigrating. Can you take over?

Yes, and it is one of the most common reasons businesses here call. The priority in the first fortnight is capturing what they know, taking control of credentials and documenting the environment before their last day, not after.

What about POPIA?

POPIA requires appropriate security for personal information and notification when it is compromised. We make sure the controls are genuinely in place and the evidence is kept. We are not attorneys and we do not give legal advice on compliance.

Are you actually based in South Africa?

Yes, we have local presence in Johannesburg alongside our Irish and UK operations, and the same standards apply across all three.
