The framework behind everything

We structure everything around
EU cybersecurity law.

The NIS2 Directive defines five pillars that every organisation must address to be genuinely secure. We use this as the backbone of how we deliver — so when a customer asks "are we compliant?", the answer isn't a guess. It's documented evidence.

Pillar 01
🗂️
Identify

Know what you have and where you're exposed. Asset inventory, risk assessment, governance policies, supply chain risk.

Why this matters for you as a director: From May 2024, NIS2 applies to a much broader set of Irish businesses than its predecessor. Directors can be held personally liable for breaches of the duty of care. We structure every engagement so you can demonstrate compliance — not just claim it.

Infrastructure management

We manage what you already have.

Every business has a different environment. Some have just laptops and a firewall. Others have servers, multiple sites, cloud infrastructure, and a network stack that's grown over years. We take on all of it.

Per-user pricing covers your people. Infrastructure — servers, firewalls, switches, access points, cloud — is quoted separately based on what you actually have. A fixed, transparent price for each asset type, assembled into a quote that reflects your specific environment.

Hardware — servers, switches, access points, laptops, cabling, UPS — is always a one-off project cost, quoted and invoiced separately from the monthly management fee.

Regardless of what your environment looks like today, we take it on as-is. No requirement to replace equipment before we start. No minimum spec. We document everything on day one, identify what needs attention, and build a plan from there.

If you want to talk through what that looks like for your specific setup, book a call — we'll walk through your environment and give you a clear picture of what's involved.

How we start

Most customers come to us with existing equipment, no Active Directory or Entra setup, and a mix of M365 licences. That's the standard starting point — not the exception.

01

Patch management automated. Security awareness training deployed. Firewall and network reviewed. A gap report shows exactly where you stand against full NIS2 compliance — and becomes the natural conversation about moving to Operate or Grow if that's where you need to go.

03

Workflow review with you and your team. Technology roadmap built around your goals. From here, your technology isn't just keeping the lights on — it's actively making your business faster, safer, and more valuable.

No Active Directory? No Entra setup? No problem.
