The problem

Most IT spending is a series of reactions.

Ask most owners what their technology plan is and the honest answer is a list of things that broke, things a supplier recommended and things a competitor seemed to have. Each decision made sense on the day. Together they do not add up to a direction.

The result is familiar: money spent in the wrong order, systems that do not talk to each other, and technology bought because it was new and not because it moved a number.

A strategy does not need to be a thick document. It needs to answer one question for every part of your technology: what does this do for earnings or risk, and where should it run to do that at the lowest total cost?

What it's worth

Every decision, looked at twice

The best owners our founder has worked with judged each technology decision twice: first as a cost or saving, then multiplied by their exit multiple.

  • It turns IT into a lever. A recurring saving of 5,000 a year is worth 30,000 at a six times multiple.
  • It stops wasted spend. Projects that do not move earnings or risk drop off the list.
  • It prepares you for diligence. A documented technology strategy is itself evidence of a well-run business.

Questions owners ask

Frequently asked questions

What is an IT strategy?

It is a plan for how technology will support the goals of the business over the next few years: what to invest in, in what order, at what cost and for what result.

What is an IT roadmap?

The roadmap is the practical part of the strategy: a timeline of initiatives with costs, owners and priorities. Ours typically look 12 to 36 months ahead and are reviewed quarterly.

Does a business our size really need an IT strategy?

If you spend money on technology, you already have one. It is just unwritten and reactive. Writing it down for a business of 20 or 100 people takes days, not months, and it pays for itself in avoided mistakes.

Is this a one-off project or ongoing?

It can be either. Many clients start with a one-off strategy and continue with quarterly reviews through our CIO as a Service.
