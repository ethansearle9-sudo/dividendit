Why It Matters

Every business owner
carries two risks.

That you never realise the value of what you spent years creating. And that a cyber incident compromises it before you get the chance. The six numbers below are those two risks, measured. Not all of them are caused by IT — but your IT sits underneath each one, and it is the factor most owners never examine.We take that second risk off your desk entirely, and make sure your IT is never the reason the first one happens to you.

70%
of owners never achieve a successful exit
Most business owners never realise the value they've created. Technology misalignment and poor exit preparation are among the most common — and preventable — reasons why.
Source: Business Exit Survey, Pepperdine University
33%
of family businesses reach a second generation
Only 15% reach the third. A business only the current owner knows how to run is a large part of why the handover fails.
Source: PwC & family business research
49%
of businesses don't make it past year five
Half of all new businesses are gone within five years. Getting the technology foundations right from day one dramatically improves the odds of survival and growth.
Source: US Bureau of Labor Statistics, Business Employment Dynamics
29%
of businesses suffered a successful cyber attack last year
Nearly a third of businesses were hit by at least one successful attack in the past 12 months. This is not a large-company problem — and the difference between a blocked attempt and a bad month is usually whether anyone was monitoring.
Source: Hiscox Cyber Readiness Report 2026
3×
SMBs are more likely to be targeted than large firms
SMBs account for over 50% of all reported cyber attacks despite representing a fraction of economic output. Criminals go where defences are weakest, which is usually where nobody owns the problem full-time.
Source: Verizon DBIR 2024
$52k
Average annual cost of cyber incidents per business — before the hidden costs
Most businesses focus on the recovery cost. The full impact is far higher once you add fraud, downtime and lost revenue — most of which is avoidable with the right controls in place beforehand.
Source: Hiscox Cyber Readiness Report 2026

None of this is inevitable. But the odds don't improve on their own — and which of these numbers matters most to you depends entirely on where your business is right now.
