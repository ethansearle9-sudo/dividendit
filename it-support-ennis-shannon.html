The Clare problem

The choice here is usually one local individual, or a provider two hours away who never comes.

Clare has more serious industry than its population suggests. The Shannon Free Zone put aviation, aerospace services and manufacturing on the doorstep, and there is a long tail of distribution, hospitality and professional businesses around Ennis.

What it does not have is depth of IT provision. The realistic options tend to be a capable local individual who is excellent but is one person, or a larger provider based in Limerick, Galway or Dublin for whom Clare is the edge of the map and site visits keep slipping.

Neither is wrong. Both leave the same gap: when something serious happens outside office hours, or when the one person who knows your systems is unavailable, there is no second line.

What works here is remote-first support that is genuinely fast, backed by a team, with someone who actually turns up when it matters.

Ennis and Shannon has its own page because the local argument is different here. The wider picture is on our outsourced IT support across Ireland, the UK and South Africa page.

Questions owners ask

Frequently asked questions

Do you actually come to Ennis and Shannon, or is it remote only?

Both. Most issues are resolved remotely and considerably faster that way, which matters more in Clare than anywhere. When something needs hands on hardware we come out, to Ennis, Shannon, the Free Zone and across the county.

We are a small business in Clare. Are we too small?

If you have roughly ten or more people and an IT failure costs you money, no. Below that, honestly, a managed service is often more than you need and we will tell you so.

Our IT is one local person who has been with us years. Why change?

You may not need to. Co-managed is usually the better answer: they stay, keep the relationship with the business, and stop being the only one who knows anything. They get cover for leave and out of hours, and you stop carrying single-person risk.

Is cyber crime really a problem for businesses in Clare?

Attacks are not geographic. Most are automated or opportunistic, and invoice fraud in particular targets any business that pays suppliers by email. Being in a smaller town is not protection.

What does it cost?

Per user, per month, with tools included, depending on headcount and what is already in place. Twenty minutes gives an honest number, and contracts roll monthly.
