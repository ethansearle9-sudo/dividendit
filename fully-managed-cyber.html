Fully managed cyber security

You run the business. We run the security: the tools, the monitoring, the response and the reporting, under one partner and one predictable monthly fee. The problem

Most businesses have security products. Very few have security.

Over the years a typical business collects security in pieces: antivirus from one supplier, a firewall from another, backup from a third. Each was a sensible purchase at the time. Nobody owns the whole picture, and nobody is watching at 2am on a Saturday.

That is where incidents happen: in the gaps between products and in the hours when no one is looking. When something does go wrong, each supplier points at the next.

Fully managed cyber security replaces the patchwork with one partner who runs the whole programme and answers for the outcome. It is the same set of controls a large company would have, sized and priced for a business like yours.

What's included

One programme, every layer covered

✓

Sign-in protection

Multi-factor sign-in enforced on every account, conditional access rules and regular access reviews, so a stolen password is never enough on its own.

✓

Managed detection and response

Modern protection on every laptop and server, with a security team looking at the alerts and acting on them.

✓

Email security

Filtering for phishing and impersonation, plus the authentication records that stop criminals sending email as your business.

✓

Patching and vulnerability management

Updates applied on a schedule across every device and application, with a report of anything outstanding.

✓

Backup and recovery

Separate, protected copies of your Microsoft 365 data, devices and servers, restored on a test basis so you know they work.

✓

Training and reporting

Practical awareness training for your team, and a monthly report for you that says what was found, what was done and what it means.

How it works

What it's worth

Security that shows up in the numbers

We look at every security decision twice: what it costs, and what it does to the risk a buyer, lender or insurer would price into your business.

  • It protects earnings. One diverted payment or a week of downtime can cost more than years of prevention.
  • It supports insurance. You can answer an insurer's security questions accurately, with evidence behind each answer.
  • It stands up in due diligence. A documented security record removes one of the common reasons a buyer chips the price or slows a deal.
Is it right for you?

An honest fit check

A good fit if

  • You have no dedicated security person, or IT is one person's side job
  • You want one partner accountable instead of several suppliers
  • You want a predictable monthly cost with the tools included
  • You want reporting you can read without a translator

Probably not the right fit if

  • You have an internal IT team that wants to stay in control day to day. Co-managed will suit you better
  • You are looking for a single product rather than a managed service
  • You have fewer than ten people. We may not be the most cost-effective fit
Questions owners ask

Frequently asked questions

What is included in managed cyber security services?

The security tools, the people who monitor them, response when something suspicious happens, patching, backup, staff awareness training and monthly reporting. It is priced per user per month with the tools included.

Is someone really watching around the clock?

Every service level includes managed detection and response on your devices. Our Operate level adds active incident response, and our Grow level adds a full 24/7 security operations centre. We recommend the level that matches your risk, and you can see what each includes on our services page.

Do we have to replace the security tools we already have?

Not necessarily. We take on your existing equipment as it is and tell you honestly which tools are worth keeping and which are duplicating each other.

Are we tied into a long contract?

No. Our agreements roll monthly. We would rather earn the relationship each month than hold you to a term.
