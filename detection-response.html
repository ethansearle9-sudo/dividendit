Our managed Detection & Response service uses SIEM (Security Information & Event Management) and SOAR (Security Orchestration, Automation & Response) to monitor your entire environment in real time — and respond to threats automatically, before they escalate.

24/7 log collection and correlation across your entire environment — detecting anomalies that indicate compromise.

Automated Response (SOAR)
