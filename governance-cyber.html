The problem

When someone asks you to prove it, can you?

Sooner or later every business gets the question. A large customer sends a security questionnaire. An insurer wants detail before renewal. A buyer's adviser asks for your policies and incident history. A regulator asks what measures are in place.

Many well-run businesses do sensible things about security and have very little written down. In the moment that counts, “we take security seriously” is a claim. A documented, reviewed, evidenced programme is proof.

Regulation is moving the same way. In the EU, the NIS2 Directive makes management bodies responsible for approving and overseeing cyber risk measures in the sectors it covers, and it requires those organisations to manage security in their supply chain. That reaches smaller suppliers through customer contracts even when the law does not apply to them directly.

What it's worth

Proof is an asset

Governance turns good practice into something you can show. That has a direct effect on sales, insurance and what the business is worth.

Questions owners ask

Frequently asked questions

What is cyber security governance?

It is how a business directs and oversees its security: who is responsible, what the rules are, how suppliers are managed, how risk is reported to leadership and how you prove all of it.

Does NIS2 apply to my business?

NIS2 applies mainly to medium and large organisations in listed sectors such as energy, transport, health, digital infrastructure, manufacturing of certain goods and others, as implemented in each EU member state. Even if you are out of scope, in-scope customers are required to manage supply chain security and may pass requirements on to you. Take legal advice on your own position.

Which security framework do you work to?

We structure security around the NIST Cybersecurity Framework: govern, identify, protect, detect, respond and recover. It is a framework we align to and report against, not a certification. We do not offer ISO 27001 certification or readiness work.

How much paperwork is involved?

Less than you fear. We keep policies short and practical. The aim is a programme that works and can be shown, not a shelf of documents nobody reads.
