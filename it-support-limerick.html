The Mid-West problem

The risk in Limerick is not cost. It is depth of bench.

Limerick has real industry behind it: manufacturing along the Raheen and Castletroy corridors, medical devices, and the businesses that supply them. These are companies where an outage stops a production line or a delivery, not just an inbox.

But the pool of experienced IT people in the Mid-West is shallower than in Dublin. That cuts both ways. Salaries are more reasonable, which is the good news. The bad news is that when your one IT person leaves, moves to a multinational on the ring road, or simply goes on holiday during a bad week, there is far less to draw on locally at short notice.

A lot of Mid-West businesses end up relying on one individual who has been there years and knows everything, none of it written down. That works right up until the day it does not.

The fix is not a bigger salary. It is cover that does not depend on one person being available.

What it's worth

Why this beats the one-person model

Most Mid-West businesses are not under-spending on IT. They are over-concentrated on one person.

  • Single-person risk disappearsThe commonest serious incident we see in regional businesses is not an attack. It is the only person who understood the system leaving.
  • Cover extends past office hoursProduction and distribution do not stop at half five, and neither do incidents.
  • Specialists instead of a generalistSecurity, networking and cloud are different skills. One hire gives you one blend of them.
  • It reads properly in a saleDocumented and outsourced to an accountable provider is a far better answer in due diligence than one trusted individual.

Questions owners ask

Frequently asked questions

Do you come on site in Limerick?

Yes. Most issues are solved remotely and considerably faster, but when something needs hands on hardware we come to you, across Limerick city, Raheen, Castletroy, the National Technology Park and the Shannon corridor.

We already have an IT person in Limerick. Does that rule us out?

No, and co-managed is often the better fit regionally. Your person keeps the relationship with the business and stops being the single point of failure. They get cover for nights, weekends and leave, and someone to escalate to.

Is IT support cheaper outside Dublin?

Salaries are more reasonable in the Mid-West, so the gap between hiring and outsourcing is narrower on paper. The thing that does not change is depth: one person is still one person, and the replacement pool here is smaller.

Do you understand manufacturing environments?

Yes. The difference that matters is that downtime has an immediate cost rather than an inconvenience cost, so monitoring, patching windows and backup testing have to be planned around production rather than imposed on it.

What does it cost?

Per user, per month, tools included. It depends on headcount and what is already in place. Twenty minutes and a look at your setup gives an honest number, and contracts roll monthly.
