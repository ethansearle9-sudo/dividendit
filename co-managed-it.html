The problem

One IT person cannot be everything.

A good internal IT person is worth a great deal. They know your people, your systems and your history. They are also expected to be a help desk, a network engineer, a security analyst, a cloud architect and a project manager, often all in the same week.

Something has to give. Usually it is the work nobody is shouting about: security, documentation, planning. And when that one person is ill or on holiday, the business is exposed.

Co-managed IT keeps what is valuable about your internal team and fills in everything one person cannot reasonably cover.

What it's worth

More from the team you already have

Co-managed IT costs far less than a second hire and removes the largest risk in a one-person IT function: the one person.

  • It protects against key-person risk. Buyers look hard at whether IT knowledge lives in one head. Now it does not.
  • It frees your IT person for higher-value work. Less firefighting, more improvement of the systems that drive the business.
  • It keeps good people. IT staff with support, tools and specialists to learn from tend to stay.
Is it right for you?

An honest fit check

A good fit if

  • You have an IT manager or a small IT team you value
  • They are stretched thin or covering areas outside their expertise
  • You worry about what happens when they are away
  • Projects keep being pushed back by daily support

Probably not the right fit if

  • You have no internal IT. Fully managed IT is the right model
  • You are looking to outsource IT entirely over time. Tell us, and we will plan for that honestly
Questions owners ask

Frequently asked questions

What is co-managed IT?

It is a partnership between your internal IT staff and an outsourced IT team. Your people keep ownership of day-to-day IT, and the partner adds specialist skills, tools, capacity and cover.

Will you try to replace our IT team?

No. Co-managed IT only works when your internal team trusts us, so we work to make them more effective. The split of responsibilities is written down and agreed with them.

How do you divide responsibilities?

It varies by business. A common split is that your team handles user support and on-site work, and we handle monitoring, patching, security, backup and escalations. We agree it together and review it regularly.

Can we use you just for projects or holiday cover?

Yes. Some clients start with a defined project or cover arrangement and extend from there once they have seen how we work.
