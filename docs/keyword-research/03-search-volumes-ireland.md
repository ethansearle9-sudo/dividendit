Source: Google Ads Keyword Planner, "Get search volume and forecasts",
Ireland, all languages, Google only. Pulled 2026-11-13 (plan ID 1445357873,
saved in the Dividend IT Ads account).

**Caveat stated up front:** with no active campaign, Keyword Planner reports
banded ranges (0–10, 10–100, 100–1k) rather than exact figures. The bands are
wide. They are still decisive here, because the gaps between options are
larger than the bands.

## Finding 1: the whole Irish MSP keyword market is tiny

Only two terms in the entire set clear 100 searches a month, and neither is a
core service term: *cyber security ireland* and *it company dublin*.

**Every head term the site is built around — "IT support Dublin", "managed IT
services Ireland", "IT support Ireland" — sits in the 10–100 band.** That is
somewhere between 120 and 1,200 searches a *year*, split across nine or more
entrenched competitors, an AI Overview, and the ads above them.

"Outsourced IT support Ireland", the exact phrase in the site's main pillar
title, returns **no measurable volume at all.**

## Finding 2: the head terms are shrinking fast

*it support dublin* and *it company dublin* both show **−90% year on year.**

Two plausible causes, and they compound rather than compete: AI Overviews
absorbing the query so it never reaches a results page, and buyers shifting
to asking an assistant instead of searching. Either way, the trend on the
terms we have been optimising towards is sharply down, and the Search Console
baseline (351 impressions in 90 days, position 57.6) is consistent with it.

**This is the strongest possible argument for the AEO work.** Ranking on a
collapsing term is worth less every month. Being the source an answer engine
cites is not subject to the same decay.

| Competitors on page 1 | 9 entrenched Irish MSPs | EY, Deloitte, law firms — **no MSP serving SMBs** |
| CPC (what advertisers pay) | €4.70 – €10.87 | no data |
| Our authority | none | none |

**Identical traffic. A fraction of the competition. Opposite trend lines.**

*technology due diligence* tells the same story: 10–100, Medium competition,
and **+∞ on both the three-month and year-on-year change** — the term went
from no measurable volume to measurable volume inside the last year. It is a
market forming right now, and the SERP check found no Irish MSP in it.

## Finding 4: the cost pages are chasing almost nothing

*how much does outsourced it cost* and *outsourced it cost* are both **0–10 a
month.** *managed it services cost ireland* has no data at all. Competitors
including ers.ie, landmark.ie, radium.ie and datapac have all published
detailed Irish price guides for this.

The cost content is still worth having — it is genuinely useful, it is the
question every buyer asks on a call, and it is the kind of specific,
numbers-led page answer engines cite. But it should be understood as **AEO
and sales-enablement content, not a traffic play.** Nobody is searching it in
volume.

## Finding 5: where the volume actually is

*cyber security ireland* (100–1k, Medium) is the single biggest term tested
and is **five to ten times** any IT-support term. *cyber security dublin* and
*cyber risk assessment* sit behind it at 10–100 with only Medium competition —
notably softer than *it support dublin* at High.

The site already has deep cyber content: `/outsourced-cyber`, `/cyber-risk`,
`/cyber-risk-assessment`, `/true-cost`, `/cyber-insurance`,
`/been-hacked-first-24-hours`, plus the incident guides. The Search Console
baseline shows the only thing Google currently associates the site with is
security — 21 of 27 queries. **That is not a problem to correct. It is the
one signal we have, and it points at the larger market.**

## What this changes

1. **Stop treating "IT support Dublin" as the prize.** 10–100 a month,
   High competition, down 90%, nine incumbents. It is the worst trade
   available. Keep the location pages — they serve buyers who do search
   that way, and they are needed for local entity signals — but they are
   not where effort should go.
2. **Lead with cyber security.** It is 5–10× the volume, the competition is
   softer, and it is already the only thing the site is known for.
3. **Own the due diligence and owner-value terms now, while they are
   forming.** Same volume as the head terms, Low to Medium competition,
   sharply rising, and no MSP competitor anywhere on those SERPs.
4. **Treat the cost and decision content as citation bait**, not traffic.
   Low volume, high usefulness, exactly the shape answer engines extract.

The two previous documents in this folder concluded that the owner-value
angle was uncontested. The volume data now shows it is uncontested *and*
growing, while the alternative is contested and collapsing. That moves it
from "interesting differentiator" to "the obvious strategy".
