## What this says

**21 of 27 queries are penetration-testing variants, and they account for
roughly 85% of all impressions.** Penetration testing is one page
(`/pen-testing`) and it is not the business. The business is outsourced and
managed IT support for owner-led companies of 10–150 people.

**Zero impressions, across 90 days, for every term the site is actually
built around:** "IT support Dublin", "outsourced IT", "managed IT services",
"IT support Limerick/Galway/Ennis", and every industry term. The 11 location
and industry pages have earned nothing.

**Two readings are possible and the data cannot yet separate them:**

1. The target terms are wrong — pattern-transferred from the 2019 LanDynamix
   Johannesburg matrix and never validated for Ireland.
2. The pages are simply too new and too weak to surface at all. They were
   first crawled in the week of 9 November 2026, and average position across
   what *does* show is 57.6 — nowhere.

Reading 2 is the more likely primary cause: a page that does not rank at all
registers no impressions regardless of how good the keyword is. But it does
not rule out reading 1, and the two have opposite remedies. **External search
volume data is required to separate them** — that is step 2 of this research.

The three genuinely interesting outliers are `syspro` (position 10),
`outsource endpoint security` (position 22) and `cyber risk assessment
companies`. SYSPRO is an ERP used in manufacturing and distribution, which is
exactly the target vertical, and position 10 is the best position on the site.
That is a signal worth pulling on.
