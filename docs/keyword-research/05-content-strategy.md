# Content strategy: outsourced IT and cybersecurity

Built 2026-11-17. Queries brainstormed from the owner-manager's point of
view, then measured in Google Ads Keyword Planner for **Ireland and the UK**
(Nov 2025 – Oct 2026, plan `1445910149`), then mapped to HubSpot's
attract / engage / delight model.

**Volume and competition are Google's. Conversion propensity is my judgement
and is labelled as such — it is the column that stops us chasing big numbers
that never become customers.**

---

## The headline: the volume is in cybersecurity, at the awareness stage

We have spent two months optimising the outsourced-IT side at the decision
stage. That is the smallest, most contested corner of the map.

| | Ireland | UK |
|---|---|---|
| `it support dublin` (decision, IT) | 10 – 100, **High** | — |
| `data breach` (awareness, cyber) | **1k – 10k**, Low | **10k – 100k**, Low |
| `ransomware` (awareness, cyber) | 100 – 1k, Low | 1k – 10k, Low |
| `business continuity plan` | 100 – 1k, Low, **+900%** | 1k – 10k, Low |

**Cybersecurity awareness terms are 10× to 100× the IT decision terms, in both
markets, at Low competition.** And Search Console already says the only thing
Google associates this site with is security.

## Part 1 — The owner's actual queries

Owners do not search in IT language. They search the symptom.

### Outsourced IT

**Awareness** — *"something is wrong and I don't know it's IT"*
> "my IT guy is leaving" · "business continuity plan" · "what is an MSP" ·
> "computer support for business" · "how much does downtime cost"

**Consideration** — *"it's IT. What are my options?"*
> "IT outsourcing" · "IT support for small business" · "co-managed IT" ·
> "IT manager salary" · "IT support cost" · "managed IT services pricing"

**Decision** — *"which provider?"*
> "IT support companies" · "IT support [city]" · "managed IT services pricing"

### Cybersecurity

**Awareness** — *"something frightening happened, or might"*
> "data breach" · "ransomware" · "phishing email" · "email hacked" ·
> "business email compromise" · "what is cyber security" · "NIS2"

> "cyber essentials" · "penetration testing" · "cyber risk assessment" ·
> "managed cyber security" · "security awareness training" ·
> "endpoint detection and response" · "Microsoft 365 backup"

**Decision**
> "cyber security companies" · "cyber security consultant"

## Part 3 — The rule this produces

> **Chase business-qualified awareness terms, not big awareness terms.**

"Data breach" is the biggest number in this entire research — 10k–100k in the
UK at Low competition. **It is also the worst target on the list.** Most of
that volume is consumers checking whether their own details leaked, plus
people reading the news. A €10 top bid against €129 for "business email
compromise" tells you what advertisers have already learned.

The terms worth owning are the ones where **the searcher has self-identified
as a business by typing the phrase at all**:

| Term | IE | UK | Comp. | Why it qualifies the searcher |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **nis2** | 100–1k | 1k–10k | Medium | A regulation. Only businesses search it |
| **cyber essentials** | 100–1k | 1k–10k | Medium | A business certification |
| **business continuity plan** | 100–1k **+900%** | 1k–10k | **Low** | Only organisations write one |
| **business email compromise** | 10–100 | 100–1k | Medium | **€129.37 top bid** |
| **succession planning** | 100–1k **+900%** | 1k–10k | **Low** | Only owners |
| **it manager salary** | 10–100 | 100–1k | **Low** | Someone costing a hire |
| **what is an msp** | 10–100 | 100–1k | **Low** | Pre-purchase research |
| **it outsourcing** | 10–100 | 100–1k | **Low (UK)** | €49.05 top bid |

Real volume, low competition, and nobody searches them by accident.

## Part 4 — The strategy

### ATTRACT

**Lead with cybersecurity, not outsourced IT.** It is where the volume is, it
is what Google already associates us with, and the fear is more acute than
the inefficiency.

**Priority 1 — the compliance cluster.** `nis2` and `cyber essentials` are the
highest-propensity awareness terms with real volume in both markets, and
nobody searches them casually. We have NIS2 language scattered across service
pages but **no page that answers "what is NIS2 and does it apply to me?"**
That is the single clearest gap.

**Priority 2 — business continuity and succession**, both **+900% three-month
growth in Ireland**, Low competition, and both sit precisely on the
owner-value positioning no competitor occupies. `/succession` exists;
**there is no business continuity plan page**, and `/backup-dr` is written as
a service description rather than as the answer.

**Priority 3 — harvest the incident content we already have.** `/invoice-fraud`,
`/email-interception-man-in-the-middle`, `/how-to-spot-a-phishing-email`,
`/been-hacked-first-24-hours` are already earning impressions with no help.
`business email compromise` carries a **€129.37** top bid in Ireland — the
most commercially valuable term found anywhere in this research — and it is
the formal name for what the invoice-fraud page describes. That page should
own both phrasings.

**Do not chase** `data breach`, `what is cyber security` or `phishing email`
as traffic goals. High volume, low propensity, and the bids prove it.

### ENGAGE

Consideration is where the propensity is uniformly High and where our existing
assets are strongest — the comparison tables and the two calculators are
textbook consideration content.

- **Penetration testing** is the anomaly worth exploiting: 100–1k IE / 1k–10k
  UK, **€109.98 top bid**, and it is the **only** thing the site currently
  ranks for. We treated that as a distraction. It is an asset.
- **Endpoint detection and response** — 1k–10k UK at **Low** competition with
  a €40.88 bid. Rare combination. `/endpoint-security` exists but is written
  as a service, not as an answer to "what is EDR and do I need it?"
- **IT manager salary / outsourcing cost** — `/do-i-need-an-it-manager` and
  `/cost-calculator` already carry real figures and a comparison table. Low
  competition, high propensity. These are right; they just need the bare-term
  targeting.
- **Cyber essentials** is UK-specific and we have no page. Worth one.

### DELIGHT

Thin volume, so this is not a traffic play — it is the referral and
brand-mention engine, which is also **Level 4 of the AEO hierarchy**, our
current binding constraint.

- Case studies and named outcomes feed the information-gain requirement
  the AEO checklist asks for, which nothing else can supply.
- Reviews and directory profiles (Clutch, Cloudtango, Golden Pages) are
  page-one assets for the decision terms we cannot win on our own authority.
- Brand mentions count in AEO **without a link**, so guest contributions and
  industry commentary pay into visibility directly.

## Part 5 — What to build, in order

1. **NIS2 page** — "Does NIS2 apply to my business?" Highest-propensity gap. Both markets.
2. **Business continuity plan page** — +900% growth, Low comp, owner-framed, no page exists.
3. **Rework `/invoice-fraud`** to own "business email compromise" as well. €129 bid.
4. **Cyber Essentials page** — UK-specific, 1k–10k UK, no page.
5. **Rework `/endpoint-security`** to answer "what is EDR" — 1k–10k UK at Low comp.
6. **Re-target the pillar pages on bare terms** (`it support`, `managed it services`, `it outsourcing`) for both markets, not geo-modified variants.
7. **`/it-support-hampshire`** — the only location gap with real volume.

## Caveats

- Advertiser competition is not organic difficulty. **The UK SERPs still have
  not been read** — that remains the unverified assumption.
- Propensity ratings are my judgement, informed by bid levels. Bids are good
  evidence (advertisers have conversion data we do not), but they are not
  proof.
- Keyword Planner bands are wide without an active campaign. Ratios between
  bands are more reliable than the bands themselves.
