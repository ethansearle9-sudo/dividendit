# AEO Content Optimization Checklist

HubSpot Academy — AEO Fundamentals certification, bonus resource. Verbatim
checklist items. Source: HubSpot Academy, © HubSpot.

This is the scoring rubric `tools/aeo_audit.py` implements. Select a page,
check off each item it currently meets; unchecked items are the optimisation
opportunities.

---

## 1. Answer-first structure

*AI systems extract content at the passage level. Pages that lead with a clear, direct answer are significantly more likely to be cited.*

- [ ] The page opens with a direct answer to the primary question it addresses, **within the first 40 to 60 words**.
- [ ] The opening answer is **self-contained** — a reader (or AI system) could understand it without reading the rest of the page.
- [ ] Supporting detail, context and background appear **after** the answer, rather than before it.
- [ ] Each major section leads with its key point before expanding into explanation or evidence.

## 2. Headers and page structure

*Descriptive, question-based headers act as extraction signals and help AI systems identify which passages answer which queries.*

- [ ] The title tag clearly frames the question or topic the page answers and is **under 60 characters**.
- [ ] The H1 accurately reflects the page's primary topic or question.
- [ ] H2 and H3 headers are descriptive and specific rather than vague — "How long does SEO take to show results?" instead of "Timeline".
- [ ] Headers include **question-phrased versions** of the queries the target audience would type into an AI tool.
- [ ] The heading hierarchy is logical and consistent (H2 for major sections, H3 for subsections).

## 3. Section independence

*AI systems extract individual passages, not full pages. Each section should hold up on its own when pulled out of context.*

- [ ] Each H2 section makes sense as a stand-alone answer without requiring the reader to have read previous sections.
- [ ] Key terms and concepts are defined **where they first appear in each section**, not only in the introduction.
- [ ] Sections don't rely on transition phrases like "as mentioned above" or "building on the previous point" for comprehension.
- [ ] A reader could land directly on any section from a search result or AI citation and immediately find useful, complete information.

## 4. Query fan-out coverage

*AI tools break questions into sub-queries and seek answers to each one individually. Pages that address the natural follow-up questions capture more citation opportunities.*

- [ ] The page addresses not just the primary question but the **natural follow-up questions that branch off from it**.
- [ ] An FAQ section is included where appropriate, with each Q&A pair written as a self-contained, extractable response.
- [ ] FAQ entries address **specific sub-questions** rather than restating the same point in different ways.
- [ ] Sub-questions are surfaced through H2 or H3 headers, giving AI systems **multiple extraction points per page**.

## 5. Definitions, lists, and scannable formatting

*Structured, extractable formats like concise definitions, lists, and comparison tables make content easier for AI systems to parse and cite.*

- [ ] Key terms and concepts are defined in clear, stand-alone sentences when first introduced.
- [ ] Bulleted or numbered lists are used to present processes, steps, or grouped items rather than burying them in paragraph form.
- [ ] **Comparison tables are used where the content evaluates or contrasts two or more options, tools, or approaches.**
- [ ] Lists and tables are labelled with descriptive headers so their context is clear when extracted independently.

## 6. Credibility and information gain signals

*AI systems prioritize content that provides unique value and comes from credible, identifiable sources.*

- [ ] The page includes **at least one element of original value not available on competing pages** — proprietary data, firsthand experience, a unique example, or a distinctive analysis.
- [ ] The content is attributed to a **named author with a visible byline**.
- [ ] The author's credentials or relevant experience are visible on the page (bio, role, linked profile).
- [ ] Claims and statistics are sourced and, where appropriate, linked to their original source.
- [ ] The content reflects genuine expertise rather than surface-level coverage assembled from other sources.

## 7. Freshness and maintenance

*Answer engines favor content that's accurate and current. Freshness signals build trust with both AI systems and human readers.*

- [ ] The page displays a **visible publish date**.
- [ ] The page displays the **most recent update date** (if revised since publication).
- [ ] All statistics, data points and benchmarks on the page are current and accurate.
- [ ] Examples, screenshots and tool references reflect current versions and practices.
- [ ] The page is scheduled for periodic review — **at least quarterly for high-priority content**.

## 8. Audience alignment and product connection

*AI systems personalize responses using context about the person asking. Content that speaks to a defined audience and connects to a relevant product is more likely to surface in personalized results and be recommended as a solution.*

- [ ] The content addresses a **clearly defined audience** rather than trying to speak to everyone.
- [ ] Language, examples and scenarios reflect the specific context of that audience — industry, role, company stage, or use case.
- [ ] The **meta description summarises the page's core answer** in plain, specific language.
- [ ] The page includes at least one **natural, non-promotional reference** connecting the topic to your product or service.
- [ ] Images include descriptive alt text that helps AI systems understand visual content.
