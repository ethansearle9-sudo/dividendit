## Video: Welcome to the AEO fundamentals certification

Search has changed. Your audience isn't just typing keywords into Google anymore. They're asking questions and getting direct answers from AI-powered tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overviews. And the brands showing up in those answers didn't get there by accident.

This certification teaches how to build a content strategy that earns visibility in both traditional search results and AI-generated answers.

**The five lessons:**

1. **Rethinking what visibility means** in the age of AI-powered search — how answer engines changed the way people find information and what that means for strategy.
2. **Establishing your AEO foundation** — the EEAT framework and how its principles inform a balanced SEO and AEO approach.
3. **Planning content for both search and AI visibility** — keyword research and a content plan that targets rankings and AI citations at the same time.
4. **Structuring content so answer engines can extract and cite it** — answer-first formatting, descriptive headers, and auditing existing pages for AEO readiness.
5. **Measuring performance across both channels** — AEO metrics including AI visibility, share of voice, citations, and referral traffic, and the real limitations of today's tools.

Every lesson has a Resources tab with downloadable transcripts, slide decks, and bonus materials.
