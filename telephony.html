The problem

Telephony fails at the seams.

When call quality is poor, who do you ring? The phone provider says it is your internet connection. The internet provider says it is your network. Whoever looks after the network says it is the handsets. Meanwhile your customers are hearing every second word.

Many businesses are also still running a phone system in a cupboard that nobody dares touch, on traditional lines that are being retired in many countries.

When one team runs your IT and your telephony, there are no seams. One team is accountable for the whole chain, and call quality is monitored like everything else we manage.

Questions owners ask

Frequently asked questions

What is a cloud phone system?

A cloud phone system, sometimes called a cloud PBX or VoIP, delivers your business phone service over the internet. There is no phone hardware in your office, and people can make and receive calls on a desk phone, computer or mobile.

Can we keep our existing phone numbers?

In almost all cases, yes. Numbers can be transferred to the new system, and we manage that process so that there is no gap in service.

Can we use Microsoft Teams as our phone system?

Yes. Teams can make and receive external calls, which suits many businesses. Where call handling is more complex, a dedicated cloud phone system integrated with Teams is often the better choice.

What is unified communications?

It means bringing calls, messaging, video meetings and presence into one system and one experience, as opposed to separate tools for each.

What happens if our internet goes down?

Calls can be set to divert automatically to mobiles or another site, and people can carry on using the app on mobile data. Your customers still get through.
