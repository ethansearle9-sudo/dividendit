Why FMCG is different

Your calendar is set by someone else, and it does not move.

An FMCG business does not control its own deadlines. Retailer ordering windows, promotional dates and seasonal peaks are fixed by customers whose scale means they do not reschedule. If your systems are down during a window, the window still closes.

That creates a shape most IT providers handle badly. Demand is not even. There are weeks where an outage is survivable and weeks where an hour is not, and the provider needs to know which is which. A generic support arrangement treats every week the same.

There is also traceability. Food producers have to be able to say what went into a batch and where it went, quickly, when asked. That data lives in systems, and if those systems or their backups fail, a recall becomes dramatically harder to manage at the exact moment you cannot afford it to be.

What is needed is a provider who knows your calendar and treats peak weeks differently.

Questions owners ask

Frequently asked questions

Can you work around our peak season?

Yes, and it is the first thing we ask for. Peaks and promotional windows go into the plan, changes are frozen around them, and support is weighted towards the weeks where an hour actually matters.

Our day starts at 5am. Does support cover that?

Yes. Early starts are normal in food and FMCG, and covering them is one of the clearest arguments for a team rather than one person.

How does this help with traceability and recalls?

By making sure the systems holding batch and production records are properly backed up and genuinely restorable, and that access to them is controlled. We are not a food safety consultancy. We make sure the data you rely on is there and correct when you need it.

Is payment fraud a real risk for food producers?

Yes. Any business paying a lot of supplier invoices by email is a target. A criminal reads a mailbox, waits for a real invoice and asks for bank details to be changed. Multi-factor sign-in and a call-back rule stop most of it.

What does it cost?

Per user, per month, tools included, depending on headcount and what is in place. Twenty minutes gives an honest number, and contracts roll monthly.
