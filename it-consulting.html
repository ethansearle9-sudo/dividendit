The problem

IT projects usually go wrong before they start.

An office move. A new server. A migration to Microsoft 365. A replacement for the system the business runs on. These are occasional, high-stakes pieces of work, and most businesses do each one only once every several years.

They tend to go wrong for the same few reasons: the scope was never written down, nobody tested the way back, the supplier chose what suited them, or the work was squeezed in around daily support until it ran months late.

A project needs someone whose job it is. One person accountable for the scope, the budget, the timeline and the result.

What it's worth

Get the big decisions right the first time

A system chosen badly costs you every month for years. Independent advice at the start is the cheapest part of any project.

  • It avoids expensive mistakes. The option a supplier prefers is not always the one that is best for your earnings.
  • It protects operations. Change is planned around the business, with a tested fallback.
  • It leaves you stronger. Each project ends with better documentation and a simpler environment than before.
Is it right for you?

An honest fit check

A good fit if

  • You are moving office or opening a new site
  • A server, system or network is due for replacement
  • You are choosing a new platform and want advice that is not a sales pitch
  • Your internal team does not have the capacity for a project

Probably not the right fit if

  • You want ongoing day-to-day support. See managed IT
  • You need custom software developed. We advise on and implement business systems, we do not build them
Questions owners ask

Frequently asked questions

What does an IT consultant do?

An IT consultant gives independent advice on technology decisions and often manages the delivery of projects, such as choosing a new system, planning a migration or redesigning a network.

How do you price projects?

Once the scope is agreed, we give you a clear price for the defined work before you commit, so there are no surprises part-way through.

Can you work alongside our existing IT provider or team?

Yes. We regularly deliver projects alongside internal teams and other providers, with responsibilities agreed at the start.

What is IT project management?

It is the planning and control of a technology project: defining the scope, scheduling the work, managing suppliers, budget and risk, and keeping everyone informed until it is finished.
