The problem

Every unresolved IT problem is someone not working.

A locked account. A laptop that will not join the meeting. A printer that has decided today is the day. None of it is dramatic, and all of it stops someone doing their job while they wait.

Too often the experience of IT support is a ticket number, a long silence and a technician who has never heard of your business asking you to turn it off and on again.

A good service desk is measured by something simple: how quickly your people get back to work, and whether they dread calling.

What it's worth

Small delays, large numbers

Support quality rarely appears on a profit and loss account, but the cost of poor support is paid every day in lost time.

  • It gives hours back. Faster fixes across a whole team add up to real capacity over a year.
  • It keeps problems away from the owner. Staff have somewhere better to go than your office door.
  • It reduces risk quietly. Clean joiner and leaver handling closes one of the most common security gaps.
Is it right for you?

An honest fit check

A good fit if

  • Your people wait too long for IT help today
  • IT problems are escalated to the owner or office manager
  • New starters are not ready to work on their first day
  • Nobody could produce a list of your devices and licences

Probably not the right fit if

  • You want a help desk to support your own customers. We support your staff
  • You only want ad hoc, pay-as-you-go support
Questions owners ask

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between an IT service desk and a help desk?

In everyday use they mean the same thing: the team your staff contact when they need IT help. A service desk usually implies a little more, including administration such as setting up new users and managing licences.

How do our staff contact the service desk?

By phone or email, with remote access so that a technician can see and fix the problem directly.

What response times can we expect?

Response and resolution targets are set out in your service agreement by priority, and we report our performance against them every month.

Can you support people working from home?

Yes. Remote support works the same wherever the person is, as long as they have an internet connection.

Will you deal with our software suppliers for us?

Yes. On our Operate and Grow levels we manage the relationship with your line-of-business software vendors, so your team has one place to call.
